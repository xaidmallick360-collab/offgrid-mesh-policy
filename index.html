Privacy Policy for OffGrid Mesh : chat offline

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Developer: Amin Chopan

Summary: OffGrid Mesh is a peer-to-peer decentralized offline messenger. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, accounts, contacts, or location data to any central server or third party.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute user privacy. OffGrid Mesh operates with zero account registration:

  • No phone numbers or email addresses are requested or required.
  • No user profiles, advertising identifiers, or analytics tracking SDKs exist in the app.
  • No central servers store your messages, voice notes, photos, or address books.

2. Cryptography & Peer-to-Peer Transmission

All communication takes place directly between nearby devices using local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth mesh routing. Every message, voice note, and image is end-to-end encrypted on your device using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption with Ed25519 digital signatures. Only the designated recipient holding the corresponding private key can decrypt your transmissions.

3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

  • Bluetooth & Nearby Wi-Fi Devices: Used strictly for peer device discovery and direct mesh packet relay. The app declares neverForLocation and does not track your physical location.
  • Microphone: Used exclusively when you touch and hold the record button to record a voice message. Audio is recorded locally and sent directly to your chosen peer.
  • Camera: Used exclusively to scan peer QR codes for instant, authenticated cryptographic friend verification.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow you to select photos you choose to send over the mesh, and to save received media to your device.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain local peer connection relays in the background so that messages can hop device-to-device even when your screen is locked.

4. Data Retention and Panic Deletion

All chats, keys, and media remain exclusively on your physical phone storage. You can permanently erase all keys, identities, messages, photos, and voice notes at any time using the in-app Panic Wipe ("Erase everything on this phone") function.

5. Children's Privacy

OffGrid Mesh does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The application is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected with a revised effective date on this page.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via your Google Play developer contact email.
