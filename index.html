Privacy Policy for OffGrid Mesh : chat offline

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Developer: Amin Chopan

Summary: OffGrid Mesh needs no account, phone number or email. Messages are end-to-end encrypted on your phone. Nearby, they travel directly between phones over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. When your phone has internet and you are set to Online, the app also uses a cloud mailbox (Google Firebase Realtime Database) to deliver encrypted messages and encrypted photos and voice notes to friends who are far away or offline. We do not run analytics or advertising and we cannot read your messages.

1. What we do not collect

  • No phone number, email address, name verification, contacts or address book.
  • No advertising identifiers and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data is collected or sent by the app.

2. Data that is stored on our cloud mailbox (only when you are Online)

  • Encrypted message envelopes addressed to a pseudonymous mailbox id derived from a device id. They contain encrypted content plus routing details (sender device id, message type, time). Envelopes are meant to be removed once delivered and after 7 days at most.
  • Encrypted photos and voice notes. Files are encrypted on your phone before upload; the key travels only inside the encrypted chat message. We cannot open them. Uploaded files are kept on the server until the developer removes them.
  • Technical data seen by the cloud provider: like any internet service, Google and Firebase can see your IP address and the time of each request. The app also contacts a Google connectivity-check address to find out whether you are online. You can switch the app to Offline at any time, and you can route cloud traffic through Tor (requires the Orbot app) in your profile.
  • Messages that stay on the nearby mesh are not sent to any server.

3. Encryption

Private chats use X25519 key exchange, HKDF and AES-256-GCM with per-message keys. Group chats use a shared AES-256-GCM group key. Control messages are signed with Ed25519. Phones that carry messages for others cannot read them. Group chats and the nearby presence announcements (your username, display name, device id and public keys) are visible to phones in range.

4. Device permissions and how they are used

  • Bluetooth and Nearby devices: find and connect to nearby phones.
  • Location: Android requires location access for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning on some Android versions. The app does not read, store or send your location.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the record button for a voice message.
  • Camera: to scan friends' QR codes and to take photos you choose to send.
  • Photos: pictures you pick are copied into the app's private storage and, when you are Online, uploaded encrypted. Received media is kept in the app's private storage and is not added to your gallery.
  • Internet: for the optional cloud mailbox and media described above.
  • Foreground service, notifications, start after reboot: keep the mesh running in the background and show new-message alerts (the alert shows who sent it, never the text).
  • Screen lock / biometrics: used to unlock the app with your phone's own face, fingerprint, PIN or pattern. The app never receives your biometric data.

5. Data stored on your phone, retention and deletion

Keys, friends, groups and chats are stored on your phone (keys in the Android Keystore-backed secure storage; chats encrypted at rest). You can delete everything with Erase everything on this phone. Disappearing-message timers remove messages from your phone only; other people keep the copies they already received. Uninstalling the app removes local data. To ask for removal of cloud-stored items, contact the developer.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Data sent through the cloud mailbox is processed by Google Firebase as our infrastructure provider.

7. Children's privacy

OffGrid Mesh does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are shown with a new effective date on this page.
